**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Their community ties give them an intelligence advantage over outsiders, and research suggests their presence acts as a powerful deterrent to would-be poachers who are reluctant to confront women."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: Their community ties / V: give / O: them an intelligence advantage over outsiders
接続: and
S: research / V: suggests / O: [that] their presence acts as a powerful deterrent to would-be poachers
関係代名詞節: who are reluctant to confront women → would-be poachers を修飾
be reluctant to do: 〜することをためらう

[ 入試ポイント ]
"be reluctant to do" は入試頻出表現。"would-be + 名詞" は「〜しようとしている」の意味で、ここでは "would-be poachers"（密猟しようとしている者）。"give A B" の第4文型と "act as" 構文も確認しておくこと。

**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Their victory was attributed to years of rigorous self-study combined with a deep passion for languages and word origins."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: Their victory / V: was attributed to / O: years of rigorous self-study
後置修飾: combined with a deep passion for languages and word origins → self-study を修飾（過去分詞句）
be attributed to A: A（原因）に起因する・帰せられる

[ 入試ポイント ]
"be attributed to" は原因を示す頻出表現。能動態 "attribute A to B"（AをBのせいにする・Bのおかげとする）も合わせて確認。"combined with" は「〜と組み合わさって」の意味を示す過去分詞句として付帯状況を表す。
