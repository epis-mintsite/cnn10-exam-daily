**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Officials confirmed that a failure in the signaling system may have allowed both trains to occupy the same track segment, triggering the devastating collision."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: Officials / V: confirmed / that節: (that) a failure in the signaling system may have allowed both trains to occupy the same track segment
修飾: triggering the devastating collision（分詞構文、結果を示す）
may have allowed = 過去の可能性を表す助動詞＋完了形

[ 入試ポイント ]
「may have + 過去分詞」は「～したかもしれない」という過去の推量を表す重要構文。allow O to do（OがするのをOKにする）のパターンと組み合わせて出題されやすい。分詞構文 triggering は結果（～して、その結果…となった）を表す。

**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "By shifting to plant-based alternatives, scientists estimate that land use could be reduced by up to 76% and water consumption could fall dramatically, offering major environmental benefits."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: scientists / V: estimate / that節: land use could be reduced by up to 76% and water consumption could fall dramatically
前置詞句: By shifting to plant-based alternatives（植物性代替品に移行することで）
分詞構文: offering major environmental benefits（結果：大きな環境上の恩恵をもたらしながら）

[ 入試ポイント ]
「By -ing」は手段・方法を示す重要表現。「could be reduced by up to X%」のように助動詞 could が仮定的可能性を示す点と、by が「割合の差」を示す点（"reduce by 76%"）がポイント。早慶高では「by」の用法（差・手段）の区別が頻出。
