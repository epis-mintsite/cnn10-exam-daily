**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Rather than simply sharing the humorous clip, these teenagers decided to harness the image's emotional energy and channel it into a series of large-scale murals dedicated to their community's struggles and hopes."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: these teenagers / V: decided / O: to harness ... and channel ...
副詞句: Rather than simply sharing the humorous clip（対比・譲歩）
O₁: to harness the image's emotional energy（不定詞）
O₂: (to) channel it into a series of large-scale murals（不定詞、並列）
修飾: dedicated to their community's struggles and hopes（過去分詞句、murals を修飾）

[ 入試ポイント ]
「rather than doing（〜するのではなく）」の構文は比較・対比の文脈で頻出。「harness A and channel it into B（Aを活かしてBへと転換する）」は英作文でも応用可能な表現。

**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:
