**英文サマリー**:

¶1 Every year in Breda, the Netherlands, thousands of people with natural red hair gather for a unique international festival called Redhead Days, or *Roodharigendag* in Dutch. What began in 2005 as a small gathering organized by a local artist has grown into one of the world's most distinctive cultural celebrations, drawing attendees from dozens of countries.

¶2 Red hair is the rarest natural hair color in the world, occurring in only about one to two percent of the global population. The trait is caused by a variant of the MC1R gene, which follows a recessive pattern of inheritance, meaning a person must receive the gene from both parents to display red hair. Scientists and geneticists view the festival as a living example of how a biological rarity can foster a unique sense of community and shared identity.

¶3 Beyond its scientific curiosity, Redhead Days carries a powerful social message about embracing individual differences and celebrating diversity. Many attendees describe feeling isolated or teased for their hair color growing up, and the festival provides a space of solidarity and pride. Organizers hope the event will inspire broader conversations about acceptance and the beauty of human variation.

**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Red hair is the rarest natural hair color in the world, occurring in only about one to two percent of the global population."

[ 構文分析 ]

[ 入試ポイント ]
**分詞構文（付帯状況）**：コンマ＋現在分詞で「〜しながら」「〜という状態で」を表す。早慶高・関西最難関高では構文分析問題として頻出。**最上級強調**：the rarest（rareの最上級）も確認。*occurring* を関係代名詞節（which occurs）に書き換えさせる問題にも注意。

**英文サマリー**:

¶1 Virtual reality television is an emerging technology that goes beyond traditional flat-screen viewing by creating fully immersive, three-dimensional environments for audiences. Using specially designed headsets and cameras capable of capturing 360-degree footage, broadcasters can now deliver content that places viewers *inside* the scene rather than merely in front of it.

¶2 The science behind VR TV relies on several key principles, including stereoscopic vision, spatial audio, and real-time rendering algorithms. The human brain naturally perceives depth by comparing the slightly different images received by each eye; VR headsets simulate this binocular disparity by displaying two slightly offset images simultaneously. Advances in processing power and reduced latency have made it possible for these systems to respond to head movements with minimal delay, preventing the nausea that plagued earlier prototypes.

¶3 While VR television opens exciting possibilities for sports broadcasting, live events, and educational content, experts caution that widespread adoption still faces significant hurdles. The high cost of production equipment, the need for fast broadband connections, and concerns about prolonged headset use—particularly for young viewers—remain challenges that the industry must overcome before VR TV can become a mainstream experience.

(195 words)

**日本語全訳**:

¶1 バーチャルリアリティ（VR）テレビは、従来の平面スクリーン視聴を超えて、視聴者に完全に没入できる立体的な環境を提供する新興技術だ。360度映像を撮影できる特殊設計のヘッドセットとカメラを使用することで、放送事業者は視聴者を画面の前に置くのではなく、シーンの「中」に置くようなコンテンツを届けることが可能になった。

¶2 VRテレビの科学的原理は、立体視覚、空間オーディオ、リアルタイムレンダリングアルゴリズムなど、いくつかの重要な概念に基づいている。人間の脳は、左右の目がわずかに異なる画像を受け取ることで奥行きを自然に知覚する。VRヘッドセットは、わずかにずらした2枚の画像を同時に表示することで、この両眼視差をシミュレートする。処理能力の向上と遅延時間の短縮により、これらのシステムが頭の動きにほぼ即時に反応できるようになり、初期のプロトタイプで問題となっていた乗り物酔いのような不快感を防ぐことが可能になった。

¶3 VRテレビはスポーツ中継、ライブイベント、教育コンテンツなど多くの可能性を開くものの、専門家は普及への障壁がまだ大きいと指摘する。制作機器の高コスト、高速ブロードバンド接続の必要性、そして特に若い視聴者にとってのヘッドセット長時間使用への懸念など、VRテレビが主流となるためには業界が乗り越えなければならない課題が残っている。

**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "The human brain naturally perceives depth by comparing the slightly different images received by each eye; VR headsets simulate this binocular disparity by displaying two slightly offset images simultaneously."

[ 構文分析 ]
文①：S: The human brain / V: perceives / O: depth
修飾①: naturally（副詞）/ by comparing ...（手段の副詞句）/ the slightly different images received by each eye（過去分詞による後置修飾）
文②（セミコロンで等位接続）：S: VR headsets / V: simulate / O: this binocular disparity
修飾②: by displaying ...（手段の副詞句）/ simultaneously（副詞）

[ 入試ポイント ]
**セミコロン（;）による接続**：関西最難関高では「セミコロンで繋がれた2文の論理関係」を説明させる設問が頻出。ここでは前半で「人間の視覚の仕組み」、後半でその「技術的模倣」を示す因果・対応関係。**過去分詞による後置修飾**（images received by each eye）も和訳問題の頻出ポイント。
