**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Unlike older screen-reading software that only processed on-screen text, this new generation of AI can interpret complex visual environments, helping users identify faces, read menus, and navigate unfamiliar spaces with far greater independence."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: this new generation of AI / V: can interpret
目的語: complex visual environments
分詞構文: helping users identify faces, read menus, and navigate unfamiliar spaces（結果・付帯状況）
比較: with far greater independence（比較級 far + greater）
従属節: that only processed on-screen text（関係詞節、screen-reading softwareを修飾）

[ 入試ポイント ]
①Unlike A, B構文（AとBの対比）は早慶・関西最難関の英文和訳で頻出。②分詞構文helping … が「結果」「付帯状況」の両義を持つ点に注意。③far + 比較級で「はるかに〜」の強調表現。④動詞の並列 identify / read / navigate の並列構造を整理して訳すこと。

**KEY SENTENCE** 🔍:

> "Despite these obstacles, the rider relied on determination and the support of local communities, who often provided food, shelter, and encouragement along the way."

[ 構文分析 ]
S: the rider / V: relied on
目的語: determination and the support of local communities（並列名詞句）
関係詞節: who often provided food, shelter, and encouragement（local communitiesを先行詞とする非制限用法）
副詞句: Despite these obstacles（逆接・譲歩）/ along the way（道中で）

[ 入試ポイント ]
①Despite + 名詞句 = 「〜にもかかわらず」（although / in spite of と同義）は書き換え問題頻出。②関係代名詞whichとwhoの区別：先行詞がlocal communities（人）なのでwhoを使用。③rely on（〜に頼る）= depend on / count on と同義で書き換え問題に出やすい。
